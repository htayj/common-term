* Cancellation and lock ordering
CLOSE-PTY publishes its cancellation request under a separate short-held mutex
before waiting for the transport mutex. Publication releases the cancellation
mutex before transport acquisition. I/O may acquire cancellation while holding
transport; no production path holds cancellation while waiting for transport.
Process polling/signalling uses the process mutex after transport release.
Concurrent closers invalidate and close the master exactly once under transport
ownership; process wait/kill operations serialize their individual state access.

Active and queued readers/writers check cancellation before another poll slice.
The master descriptor is protected through poll and read/write, preventing close
and descriptor reuse during the native call. Active reads return EOF; active
writes return their accepted prefix. Calls entering after publication signal the
typed closed condition. Polling slices are at most 50ms; writer deadlines retain
the default two-second bound and timeout zero makes a nonwaiting attempt.

Saver DETACH-PTY invalidates copied ownership and replaces transport, process and
cancellation mutexes without acquiring inherited mutexes. The disposable fork
regression deliberately holds all three mutexes across fork; child detach and
continued parent usability pass in the actual PTY suite.

then returns EOF/zero; there is exactly one readiness invocation and one native
descriptor close. Three concurrent closers finish normally.

The 120-iteration stress uses 60 raw no-child and 60 real HUP-ignoring-child

max0.068s, p95 0.067999s, zero close+all-join measurements >=1s. Every child is

baseline. No output is printed within measured regions. The same503 checks
passed again through the final dispatcher PTY gate with identical maxima.

An additional independent original-shape reproduction opened12 raw no-child

mutex handoff fairness for the reproduced polling starvation. Catastrophic
allocation failure and arbitrary operating-system suspension were not injected.
